# Canonical consolidation v1 contract vectors

These fixtures freeze the contract-only boundary for deliberate consolidation: deriving one durable claim from an explicit set of source claims (ADR 0003, CONS-01..10). Every `.jsonl` file contains one record plus exactly one repository-framing LF; the LF is excluded from contract digests but included in the raw SHA-256 pins. None of the fixture scope, policy reference, consolidator identity, or digest fields carries runtime authority.

Consolidation is derivation, never mutation: sources are superseded or left untouched by separate lifecycle events, and no field of a source is rewritten by a consolidation statement (CONS-01). The five positive artifacts are public structural bytes. `ConsolidationRequestV1` is authority-free input: it asserts source coordinates, a policy reference, a requested output kind and modality, and authored summary text, and cannot select its authenticated scope, the live conflict state of any source, any source's kind, modality, depth, or revision, the active policy, or the derivation outcome. `ConsolidationStatementV1` binds the frozen runtime profile, server-derived scope, the exact sorted set of distinct source claim fingerprints and revisions, consolidator identity and version, policy reference and body digest, output kind and modality, output depth, the computed effective interval, and the output disposition. `ConsolidationReceiptV1` names the minted `statement_id` and commits to the exact authored summary bytes through `summary_enrichment_digest`; the summary is versioned enrichment and never enters statement identity, exactly like an embedding vector under REPLAY-01.

The derivation rules are deterministic and replay-closed. Sources are strictly sorted by claim fingerprint and must be distinct: the same fingerprint appearing twice at any revisions is the one self-cycle decidable from a single statement and is rejected at shape (CONS-09); general cycles through previously accepted derivatives require the lineage-graph witness at the repository seam. Under the only admitted v1 kind rule, sources share one assertion kind and the output kind equals it (CONS-03). The output modality must be policy-admitted and never exceeds the weakest source modality: sources may mix modalities, and v1 accepts that a derivative may summarize stronger evidence in weaker terms rather than rejecting the mix. Output depth is exactly the deepest source depth plus one and stays inside the policy bound, so lineage chains are finite. The effective interval is exactly the intersection of source intervals; an empty intersection means the sources were never co-valid and fails closed. Conflicted sources either fail closed or derive a disputed output referencing exactly the involved conflict fingerprints, strictly sorted (CONS-04): an open conflict is never laundered into an active output, and a waived conflict is tolerated only explicitly — the waiver is witnessed in the source coordinate and the disputed output preserves the reference. The statement's profile reference must name the exact frozen runtime profile compiled into the validating binary; a statement cannot mint a second identity by naming other profile digests (CONS-05, REPLAY-01).

Three digest domains thread the fixtures, all under the frozen formula `SHA-256(prefix || 0x00 || exact_bytes)` (`digest.rs` `domain_separated_digest`): the policy body digest (`ostk-consolidation-policy-v1`), the statement identity (`ostk-consolidation-statement-v1`), and the receipt's commitment to the exact authored summary bytes (`ostk-consolidation-summary-enrichment-v1`). All three are `DigestDomain` variants landed with W0-REG; a fourth, `ostk-consolidation-receipt-v1`, is reserved there for the receipt identity the seam will mint and threads no fixture here. The values carried by the fixtures are the real values computed under the frozen formula. `validate_derivation` recomputes the policy body digest through `domain_separated_digest` and rejects any statement whose `policy_digest` does not commit to the supplied policy body.

This contract grants no admission authority (CONS-02, CONS-06). The repository seam must additionally prove that the policy is the active registry body for the scope, that every source coordinate matches a live claim at the stated revision, that declared conflict states match the live conflict projection, that every source belongs to the statement's single server-derived scope with no wider-visibility content crossing into the output, and that the lineage graph stays acyclic when the new claim is linked. This module intentionally exposes no production constructor for that admitted seam.

The adversarial records each falsify exactly one rule and fail at the documented stage:

- `negative-single-source` — fewer than two sources; statement shape.
- `negative-unsorted-sources` — source set not strictly sorted by fingerprint; statement shape.
- `negative-duplicate-source-claim` — one claim fingerprint at two revisions (self-cycle); statement shape.
- `negative-profile-digest-spoof` — statement names non-frozen profile digests; statement shape.
- `negative-disputed-refs-unsorted` — disputed disposition references not strictly sorted; statement shape.
- `negative-kind-mixing` — sources mix assertion kinds; derivation (uniform kind).
- `negative-modality-promotion` — output modality stronger than the weakest source; derivation.
- `negative-modality-not-policy-admitted` — output modality outside the policy's admitted set; derivation.
- `negative-depth-exceeded` — deepest source at the policy depth bound, output beyond it; derivation.
- `negative-empty-interval` — disjoint source intervals, empty intersection; derivation fail-closed.
- `negative-conflict-laundering` — open-conflict source with an active disposition; derivation.
- `negative-disputed-without-refs` — waived-conflict source, disputed disposition missing the reference; derivation.
- `negative-disputed-ref-mismatch` — disputed disposition references a conflict no source involves; derivation.
- `negative-policy-digest-mismatch` — statement policy digest does not commit to the supplied policy body; derivation.
- `negative-normative-output` — policy admitting `normative` output; policy shape.
- `negative-request-authority-fields` — request carrying authority fields; strict decode (`deny_unknown_fields`).

Rust tests raw-pin the SHA-256 of every fixture file's exact bytes (including the trailing LF) and of the suite manifest itself, decode the manifest as a typed `deny_unknown_fields` struct, and assert every manifest digest against the recomputed file digests. Every positive fixture must re-encode through the canonical encoder to its exact checked-in bytes. Changing any canonical record, digest prefix, fixed value, expected digest, or ordering rule is a contract-version change.
